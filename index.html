Section C — Profile
Y Pengly., Phnom Penh

Born in Srey Santhor District, Kampong Cham Province, and currently based in Phnom Penh, I hold two academic backgrounds that combine technology and communication: a Bachelor of Computer Science and Engineering from the Royal University of Phnom Penh and a Bachelor of Arts in English (Major in Professional Communication) from the Institute of Foreign Languages. One developed my ability to design and build systems; the other strengthened my ability to communicate ideas clearly.

Through academic and personal projects, I have gained hands-on experience in software development, including a café management system using C#.NET and PostgreSQL, a hotel booking website using HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, PHP, and MySQL, as well as programming projects focused on C/C++, algorithms, and object-oriented design. Each project represents my approach to learning — exploring concepts, solving problems, and building practical solutions.

"Adaptable in new environments, driven by curiosity, and always looking for ways to turn technology into practical solutions."

Beyond coding, I enjoy exploring new places, connecting with people, and keeping up with emerging technologies. I am currently seeking opportunities in the IT field where I can contribute my technical skills, collaborate with a team, and continue growing as a software developer.
